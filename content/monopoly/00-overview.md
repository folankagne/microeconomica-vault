title: "Monopoly — Overview"
---
Drops [the price-taking assumption from producer theory](../producer-theory/00-overview.md): here the producer is the *only* supplier, so her own output decision moves the price she sells at. The chapter works through three increasingly permissive versions of what the monopolist can do — post one price for everyone, post different prices for different observable segments, or design a menu of quantity-price pairs that gets different consumers to *self-select* into paying different effective prices — and shows that the standard "price above marginal cost, deadweight loss" result is really a *special case* of a more general phenomenon, market power.

Source: Osborne & Rubinstein (2020), Chapter 7 (`beyond-lectures`).
