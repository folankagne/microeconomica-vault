title: "Exchange Economies — Overview"
---
Upgrades [the market model](../equilibrium-foundations/the-market-model-and-equilibrium.md) from indivisible houses to **divisible goods** — the standard general-equilibrium setting, and the natural meeting point of [consumer theory](../consumer-theory/00-overview.md) (each individual optimizes over a budget set) with a genuine market-clearing condition across many agents at once. The central chapter (10) develops competitive equilibrium, its two welfare-theorem analogues, the core, and the Edgeworth box; the remaining four chapters extend the same machinery to indivisible goods with money, uncertainty, production, and — in the two final chapters — settings where information itself is asymmetric between traders.

Source: Osborne & Rubinstein (2020), Chapters 10–14 (`beyond-lectures`).
