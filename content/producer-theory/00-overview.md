Not part of the actual course — added directly from the textbook (tagged `beyond-lectures` throughout) to round out the individual-agent side of the vault. Mirrors [consumer theory](../consumer-theory/00-overview.md) structurally: a producer transforms an input into an output via a production function, and — just as a consumer's behavior depended on which preference properties were assumed — a producer's behavior depends on *which objective* she's assumed to pursue. The chapter's central, slightly subversive point is that "maximize profit" is not the obviously-correct assumption it's often treated as; it's one of several coherent objectives, chosen partly for its analytical convenience.

Source: Osborne & Rubinstein (2020), Chapter 6.
