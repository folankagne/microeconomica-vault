title: "Equilibrium Foundations — Overview"
---
The vault's first genuinely **multi-agent** models — not `beyond-lectures` extensions of individual choice, but the first real equilibrium concepts, worked out in the simplest possible setting: $n$ individuals, $n$ indivisible houses, one house per person. Two very different allocation mechanisms are studied on exactly the same underlying object (a society with preferences over houses): **the jungle**, where raw power determines who gets what, and **the market**, where initial ownership and voluntary trade determine it. Both turn out to connect tightly to **Pareto stability** — and the market chapter, in particular, works out a fully self-contained first-and-second-welfare-theorem pair using nothing but an elegant combinatorial algorithm (top trading cycles), no calculus required.

Source: Osborne & Rubinstein (2020), Chapters 8–9 (`beyond-lectures`).
