A structured, cross-linked knowledge base for an Introduction to Microeconomics course, built around Osborne & Rubinstein's *Models in Microeconomic Theory* (2020). Part I follows the course's own lectures (Ch.1–5); Part II extends into the rest of the textbook (Ch.6–20) as a fuller reference. 66 entries, organized as a sequential reading course rather than a flat encyclopedia.
