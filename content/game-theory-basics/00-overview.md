Everything so far has modeled one decision-maker (or, in [exchange economies](../exchange-economies/00-overview.md), many decision-makers interacting only anonymously through prices). This folder introduces genuine **strategic interaction**: outcomes depend on everyone's actions jointly, and each player must reason about what the others will do. Two families of models are covered — **strategic (simultaneous-move) games**, with Nash equilibrium as the central solution concept, and **extensive (sequential-move) games**, with subgame perfect equilibrium and backward induction. A closing section specializes extensive games to **bargaining**, showing how the fine details of a negotiation protocol (a deadline, who moves first, impatience) can swing the entire predicted outcome.

Source: Osborne & Rubinstein (2020), Chapters 15–16 (`beyond-lectures`).
