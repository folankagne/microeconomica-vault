## Primary source (course)

**Introduction to Microeconomics** — M1 Economics & Psychology, Paris School of Economics / Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne. Instructor: Leonardo Pejsachowicz. Materials used: `Lecture_slides_1_ITM_2025.pdf`, `Lecture_slides_2_ITM_2025.pdf`, `Lecture slides 4 itm 2025.tex`, `Lecture slides 5 itm 2025.tex`, and the tutorial set `TD_ITM_2026_2027_V2` (TD1–TD4, TDExtra, Methodology.tex). The course covers Chapters 1–5 of the main textbook only (individual preferences and choice, uncertainty, consumer theory) — not producer theory, monopoly, general equilibrium, or game theory.

## Main textbook

Martin J. Osborne and Ariel Rubinstein, *Models in Microeconomic Theory*. Cambridge, UK: Open Book Publishers, 2020. "She" edition, version 2020.3.26. CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. DOI: [10.11647/OBP.0211](https://doi.org/10.11647/OBP.0211).

Note: the book also exists in a "he" edition with identical content but different pronouns and, more importantly, different problem numbering (per the TD project's own `references.bib`). This vault follows the "she" edition throughout, matching the course materials.

## Reference textbook (not yet drawn on)

Hal R. Varian, *Intermediate Microeconomics* (7th ed. or later) — listed as the course's reference text, not the main one.
